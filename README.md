  <h2>👨‍💻 About Me</h2>
</div>

<img src="https://capsule-render.vercel.app/api?type=rect&color=0:0ea5e9,100:8b5cf6&height=2&section=header" width="100%" alt="divider" />

I am an Informatics Engineering graduate from **Politeknik Negeri Cilacap** with a cross-functional profile that connects **software development**, **UI/UX design**, and **AI-driven data engineering**. I was recognized as one of the **top graduates of the AI Engineer Intensive Program** by Pijak in collaboration with IBM SkillsBuild and Dicoding Indonesia (2026), out of approximately 600 participants.

My work spans building Laravel and MySQL web systems, designing visual identities and interfaces in Figma, and preparing the data that powers Indonesian-language question answering systems based on **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** and a fine-tuned **IndoBERT** model. I value attention to detail, clear communication in formal and cross-functional teams, and the ability to adapt quickly to different technical requirements.
